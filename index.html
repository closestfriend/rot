Degradation Parameters

"The poor image is a copy in motion. Its quality is bad, its resolution substandard. As it accelerates, it deteriorates. It is a ghost of an image, a preview, a thumbnail, an errant idea, an itinerant image distributed for free, squeezed through slow digital connections, compressed, reproduced, ripped, remixed, as well as copied and pasted into other channels of distribution." — Hito Steyerl

Here, we do not artificially simulate decay; we perform it. We re-compress, again and again, letting the algorithm chew the data until only the artifact remains—a digital fossil of the original intent, beautifully ruined by the mechanics of its preservation.
